🚚 Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Thank you for choosing Asha RO Water. This policy explains how drinking water and services are delivered to our customers.

⚡ Instant Automated Delivery: Water is dispensed immediately on-site at the automated dispensing tap upon successful payment or RFID card tap.

1. Method of Delivery

Asha RO Water operates on-site automated Water ATMs and dispensing plants. Because drinking water is dispensed on-demand directly into the customer's container:

  • Immediate Physical Dispensing: The delivery occurs instantly at the water dispensing nozzle located at Banwariya, UP.
  • No Courier / Postal Shipping: We do not ship water through courier or postal logistics. Customers collect pure drinking water directly at the dispensing machine.

2. Delivery Timeline

Delivery is instant (within 5 to 30 seconds depending on the selected volume, e.g. 10 Litres or 20 Litres).

3. RFID Card Delivery

Customers registering for an RFID Smart Card collect their card in person at our operating center (Banwariya) upon verification.

4. Undelivered Water / Delivery Failure

If water is not dispensed after successful payment due to technical failure, our Refund Policy guarantees an immediate re-dispense or a 100% full refund back to your source bank account within 24 to 48 hours.
