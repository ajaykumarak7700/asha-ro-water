💧 About Us - Asha RO Water

Welcome to Asha RO Water (operating under Asha Digital Center), an initiative dedicated to providing pure, hygienic, mineral-enriched, and affordable drinking water to rural and semi-urban communities.

Mission: Pure, Clean & Chilled Drinking Water accessible 24/7 for everyone at just ₹1 per Litre through Smart Automation.

Who We Are

Founded by Ajay Kushwaha at Banwariya, Uttar Pradesh, Asha RO Water combines multi-stage industrial water purification (Reverse Osmosis + UV + Mineralization) with modern smart dispensing technology.

What We Provide

  • Automated 24/7 Water ATM: Customers can dispense pure water anytime using Smart RFID Cards or instant UPI scanning (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, etc.).
  • Highest Purity Standards: Multi-barrier filtration removing dissolved impurities, bacteria, and viruses while retaining essential healthy minerals.
  • Cashless Transparency: Full customer passbook, automated billing, and live transaction ledger.
