💸 Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

At Asha RO Water, customer satisfaction and trust are our top priorities. Because water is an automated consumable product, this policy outlines the conditions under which refunds are provided.

⚡ Immediate Help: If you paid through UPI or Card and water was not dispensed due to power cut or machine fault, call/WhatsApp us immediately at +91 7700828989.

1. Failed Dispensing (Water Not Dispensed)

If your account or UPI wallet was debited but the dispensing machine did not pour water due to power interruption, network delay, or sensor error:

  • Immediate Re-dispense: The operator can manually trigger the required water litres immediately.
  • Wallet Credit: If you have an RFID card, the amount will be credited back to your card wallet immediately.
  • Bank Refund: If paid directly via UPI, the full amount will be refunded directly back to your source bank account via Razorpay within 24 to 48 banking hours.

2. Cancellation of Order

Because the water dispensing starts immediately upon payment confirmation, cancellation is not possible once the motor has started pumping water into your container.

3. RFID Card Balance Refund

Customers moving away or wishing to surrender their RFID water card can request a full refund of their remaining wallet balance by contacting the operator at Banwariya.

4. How to Claim a Refund

To request a refund, please provide:

  • Your UPI Transaction Ref / Razorpay Payment ID
  • Date and Time of transaction
  • Mobile Number

Send details via WhatsApp or Call to +91 7700828989 or email to support@asharowater.in.
