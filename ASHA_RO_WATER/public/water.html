Kaise Kaam Karta Hai? (3 Easy Steps)

RO Water ATM machine par paani lene ke aasan 3 niyam:

  • 1

    Jar Nozzle Ke Niche Rakhein

    Apna saaf Jar ya Bottle dispensing tap ke theek niche rakhein.

  • 2

    Machine Par Laga UPI QR Scan Karein Ya Card Tap Karein

    PhonePe, Google Pay, ya Paytm se machine par chipka QR code scan karein aur amount pay karein (ya Smart Card tap karein).

  • 3

    Automatic Pani Bhar Jayega!

    Machine ka buzzer bajega aur motor accurate 10L ya 20L paani nikaal kar automatic band ho jayegi.
