🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

At Asha RO Water, we are committed to safeguarding the privacy and security of our customers and website visitors.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect minimal information necessary to deliver pure water services and process transactions:

  • Contact Details: Customer name, phone number, and address (for RFID card registration and recharge passbook).
  • Transaction Records: Transaction IDs, litres dispensed, amount paid, and timestamps.

2. Payment Information Security

All online payments (UPI, Cards, Net Banking) are securely processed through Razorpay Payment Gateway using end-to-end encryption complying with RBI guidelines. Asha RO Water never collects, sees, or stores any banking PINs, UPI PINs, or card CVVs.

3. Use of Information

Collected customer data is strictly used for:

  • Crediting RFID water cards and processing automated water dispensing.
  • Customer grievance resolution and refund processing.
  • System maintenance and transaction reconciliation.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any marketing agencies or third parties. Data is only shared with authorized payment partners (Razorpay) strictly to process your payment.

5. Contact Us Regarding Privacy

For any queries about your data or account records, contact us at support@asharowater.in or call +91 7700828989.
